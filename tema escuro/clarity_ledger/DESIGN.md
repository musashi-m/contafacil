## Brand & Style
The design system embodies precision, financial confidence, and nocturnal clarity for modern bookkeeping and expense tracking. Designed specifically for ambitious operators, finance leads, and independent professionals, it shifts away from overwhelming enterprise spreadsheets toward a clean, editorial, data-dense interface.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with structural **Tonal Layering** and subtle ambient glows. The visual interface prioritizes immediate legibility, rigorous visual hierarchy, and instant cognitive capture of financial health metrics. Deep navy and obsidian canvas layers minimize eye strain over extended accounting sessions, while hyper-crisp typography and intentional semantic accents turn dense ledgers into calm, actionable views.

## Colors
The color architecture rests on three tonal tiers of deep slate/navy neutrals, punctuated by purposeful semantic signaling rather than decorative color fills.

- **Canvas & Surface Tier:**
  - `Base Canvas`: `#0B0F17` (Deep Obsidian Navy for main app backdrop)
  - `Surface Level 1`: `#111827` (Card and panel backgrounds)
  - `Surface Level 2`: `#1E293B` (Elevated modules, modal sheets, and active states)
  - `Surface Level 3`: `#26354A` (Hover interactions and selected rows)

- **Border & Division:**
  - `Border Subtle`: `#1E293B`
  - `Border Vibrant`: `#334155`
  - `Border Focus`: `#10B981` (Emerald focus indicator)

- **Functional & Semantic Palette:**
  - `Emerald Positive` (`#10B981`, highlight: `#34D399`): Positive balances, settled payments, verified transactions, and growth percentages.
  - `Cyan Secondary` (`#38BDF8`): System links, secondary operational actions, and neutral data tags.
  - `Amber Pending` (`#F59E0B`, highlight: `#FCD34D`): In-flight reconciliations, pending invoices, and warning thresholds.
  - `Rose Critical` (`#F43F5E`, highlight: `#FB7185`): Overdue accounts, deficit states, and destructive interactions.

- **Typography & Icon Gradients:**
  - `Text Primary`: `#F8FAFC` (Near-white, optimum contrast on dark surfaces)
  - `Text Secondary`: `#94A3B8` (Muted cool gray for subheads and metadata)
  - `Text Tertiary`: `#64748B` (Subtle captions, timestamps, and column headers)

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single typographic family, offering crisp geometry, broad apertures, and exceptional readability at dense analytical scales.

- **Tabular Alignment:** When rendering ledger sheets, transaction rows, and balance tables, activate OpenType tabular numbers (`tnum`) across all `numeric-*` and `body-*` levels to preserve vertical decimal and integer tracking.
- **Micro-Metrics:** `label-sm` utilizes uppercase transform with `0.05em` letter tracking strictly for table headers, metadata status stamps, and badge identifiers.
- **Hierarchy Anchors:** Headlines strictly pair with `Text Primary` (`#F8FAFC`). Subheads and auxiliary metric descriptions drop directly to `Text Secondary` (`#94A3B8`) to eliminate noise.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column responsive grid on desktop, compressing to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (Margin: `1rem`, Gutter: `1rem`)
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (Margin: `1.5rem`, Gutter: `1.25rem`)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (Margin: `2.5rem`, Gutter: `1.5rem`, max width `1440px` centered)

- **Grid Alignment:** Financial summary KPIs inhabit symmetrical 3-column splits on desktop (4 columns each), collapsing to 2x2 cards on tablet and a single scroll stack on mobile.
- **Information Density:** Spacing utilizes an intentional 4px baseline rhythm (`space-xs` to `space-2xl`). High-density tabular views compress internal vertical row padding to `space-sm` (8px), maintaining rapid scanning capability without truncation.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering**, subtle outer hair lines, and concentrated directional luminance.

- **Surface Stacking:** 
  - The canvas base sits at `#0B0F17`.
  - Cards, panels, and ledger groups sit at `#111827`, bordered with a 1px continuous stroke of `#1E293B`.
  - Floating items (dropdowns, flyout balance sheets, modals) step up to `#1E293B`, framed with `#334155`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Every card container and cell segment uses crisp 1px strokes rather than heavy shadow projections. This keeps numbers sharp and borders razor-clean on OLED and high-DPI displays.
- **Ambient Focus Glows:** When cards or input modules are focused or active, apply a subtle, tinted ambient glow: `0 0 20px -5px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` for primary actions or `0 0 20px -5px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)` for secondary controls. Modals feature an overlay backdrop composed of `#0B0F17` at 75% opacity with an 8px blur filter.

## Shapes
The shape language uses balanced rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`), softening the strict tabular environment without losing architectural structure.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Standard buttons, text input fields, transaction row hover states, and dropdown menus.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Metric scorecards, modular data blocks, charts, and slide-over drawers.
- **Pill Geometry (9999px):** Applied selectively to status chips, transaction categories (e.g., "Software", "Payroll"), and filter toggles.
- **Structural Integrity:** Tables maintain squared outer bounds or sit flush inside 16px rounded outer containers with sharp internal grid lines (`#1E293B`).

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Emerald background (`#10B981`), bold slate text (`#0B0F17`), 8px border radius, medium weight. Hover shifts to `#34D399` with subtle ambient bloom.
  - *Secondary:* Dark surface (`#1E293B`), text (`#F8FAFC`), crisp border (`#334155`). Hover transitions to `#26354A` with border color `#64748B`.
  - *Ghost / Tertiary:* Transparent background, text (`#94A3B8`), hover background (`#1E293B` at 50% opacity), text brightening to `#F8FAFC`.

- **Ledger Rows & Lists:**
  - Alternating or bordered items encased in 1px borders of `#1E293B`.
  - Hover state applies background `#1E293B` with immediate transition.
  - Columns enforce tabular numeric alignment for currencies, dates, and account tags. Negative numbers highlighted in Rose (`#FB7185`), cleared settlements in Emerald (`#34D399`).

- **Input Fields & Selectors:**
  - Background `#111827`, boundary `#1E293B`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`.
  - Focus state elevates border to `#10B981` with zero interior color shift, accompanied by a 2px outer outline ring at 20% opacity.

- **Status Chips & Badges:**
  - Fully pill-shaped with tinted translucent backgrounds and high-contrast text.
  - *Settled:* Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#34D399`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
  - *Pending:* Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#FCD34D`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.
  - *Overdue / Alert:* Background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)`, text `#FB7185`, border `1px solid rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.25)`.

- **Cards & Stat Widgets:**
  - Solid `#111827` surface with 16px radius, enclosed by a 1px `#1E293B` perimeter.
  - Metrics display uses `headline-xl` in `#F8FAFC`, paired with micro-labels in uppercase `label-sm` (`#64748B`) and contextual change chips positioned top-right.

- **Checkboxes & Segmented Controls:**
  - Unchecked inputs use a 2px outline of `#334155` on `#0B0F17`.
  - Checked inputs fill with `#10B981` featuring a crisp obsidian `#0B0F17` checkmark.
  - Segmented range switchers sit inside an `#0B0F17` shell with `#1E293B` pill sliding tabs.
